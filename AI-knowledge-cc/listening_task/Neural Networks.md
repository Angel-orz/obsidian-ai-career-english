# Listening Task — Neural Networks

## Recommended Content
### Primary: 3Blue1Brown — Neural Networks
- **Channel:** 3Blue1Brown (Grant Sanderson)
- **Playlist:** "Neural Networks" (4 videos)
- **Start with:** "But what is a neural network?" (Chapter 1, ~19 min)
- **Why:** The gold standard for neural network intuition. Exceptional visual animations make backpropagation and gradient descent click. Grant's clear, measured English is perfect for non-native listeners.

### Alternative: Andrej Karpathy — "The spelled-out intro to neural networks"
- **Channel:** Andrej Karpathy
- **Video:** "The spelled-out intro to neural networks and backpropagation"
- **Duration:** ~2 hours (watch in 30-min segments)
- **Why:** Builds a neural network from scratch in Python, explaining every line. Excellent for connecting theory to code.

## What to Listen For
1. How Grant explains the layered structure — each layer as a transformation
2. The visual explanation of gradient descent: a ball rolling downhill
3. How backpropagation is presented as "attributing responsibility" for the error
