# Listening Task — Classification & Regression

## Recommended Content

### Primary: StatQuest — Logistic Regression
- **Channel:** StatQuest with Josh Starmer
- **Video:** "Logistic Regression — Clearly Explained"
- **Duration:** ~8-15 minutes
- **Level:** Beginner-friendly but thorough
- **Why:** Josh Starmer explains logistic regression with exceptional clarity — visual animations, step-by-step math, and intuitive examples. Perfect for solidifying the classification concept while practicing English listening.

### Alternative: Andrew Ng — Classification & Logistic Regression
- **Course:** Machine Learning Specialization (Coursera / YouTube)

- **Duration:** ~10-12 minutes
- **Why:** Andrew Ng's clear, measured speech is ideal for non-native English listeners. He introduces the sigmoid function and decision boundary with precise terminology used in real interviews.

## What to Listen For

1. **Terminology in context** — How does the speaker naturally use words like "decision boundary," "sigmoid," "threshold"? Listen for collocations: "draw a decision boundary," "apply the sigmoid function," "set a threshold."

2. **Explanation patterns** — Notice how good teachers explain classification vs regression. They often use contrast: "Unlike regression which..., classification..." — this is exactly how you should structure your interview answers.

3. **Transition phrases** — How does the speaker move between concepts? Listen for: "Now, the question is...", "This brings us to...", "The key insight here is..." — steal these for your own technical explanations.

4. **German pronunciation** — In the KI-Campus video, focus on: Klassifikation, Entscheidungsgrenze, logistische Regression, Wahrscheinlichkeit. Repeat each term aloud after hearing it.
