# Listening Task — CNN

## Recommended Content
### Primary: StatQuest — Convolutional Neural Networks
- **Channel:** StatQuest with Josh Starmer
- **Video:** "Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) clearly explained"
- **Duration:** ~10-15 min
- **Why:** Exceptional visual breakdown of convolution, pooling, and how CNNs work step by step.

### Supplement: DeepLearning.AI — CNN Course (Andrew Ng)
- **Platform:** Coursera / YouTube
- **Course:** Convolutional Neural Networks (Deep Learning Specialization, Course 4)
- **Start with:** Week 1 videos on convolution and padding
- **Why:** Industry-standard terminology and clear pacing for non-native speakers.

## What to Listen For
1. How the speaker explains convolution — "slide," "element-wise multiply," "sum"
2. The transition phrases between convolution, activation, and pooling
3. Terminology: "feature map," "receptive field," "stride," "padding"
