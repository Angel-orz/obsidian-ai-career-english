# K-Means

## Definition (English)
K-Means is a popular clustering algorithm that partitions data into K distinct groups by minimizing the distance between data points and their assigned cluster centers (centroids).

## Chinese
K均值 — 一种将数据分为K个不同组的聚类算法，通过最小化数据点到其所属聚类中心的距离来优化分组。

## German
der K-Means-Algorithmus

## Intuition
Think of placing K flags on a map to best serve all houses in a city. You start with random flag positions, assign each house to the nearest flag, then move each flag to the center of its assigned houses. Repeat until the flags stop moving — that's K-Means.

## Example
A retail chain uses K-Means with K=5 to identify 5 optimal store locations based on customer density, competitor proximity, and traffic patterns — each centroid represents an ideal store placement.

## Spoken Version
"K-Means finds K natural clusters in your data by iteratively adjusting cluster centers until they best represent the groupings."
