# Dimensionality Reduction

## Definition (English)
Dimensionality reduction is a technique that reduces the number of input features in a dataset while preserving as much meaningful information as possible, making data easier to visualize and faster to process.

## Chinese
降维 — 在保留尽可能多有意义信息的同时，减少数据集中输入特征数量的技术。

## German
die Dimensionsreduktion / die Dimensionsreduzierung

## Intuition
Imagine describing a 3D sculpture with a single photograph — you lose some detail, but a good photo from the right angle captures the most important features. Similarly, dimensionality reduction finds the best "angle" to project high-dimensional data into fewer dimensions.

## Example
A genomics researcher uses PCA (Principal Component Analysis) to reduce 20,000 gene expression measurements to 50 principal components, enabling visualization and clustering of cancer subtypes that would be impossible in the original high-dimensional space.

## Spoken Version
"Dimensionality reduction compresses your data by keeping the most informative features and discarding noise — it's essential for visualizing high-dimensional data and speeding up model training."

## German Workplace Example
"Wir haben eine Dimensionsreduktion durchgeführt, um die 500 Merkmale auf 20 Hauptkomponenten zu reduzieren, ohne wichtige Informationen zu verlieren."
