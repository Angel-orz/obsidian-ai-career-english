# Clustering

## Definition (English)
Clustering is an unsupervised learning technique that groups similar data points together based on their features, without using predefined labels.

## Chinese
聚类 — 将相似的数据点根据其特征自动分组的技术。

## German
das Clustering / die Clusteranalyse

## Intuition
Imagine you have a pile of mixed Lego bricks. Clustering is like sorting them by color and shape without anyone telling you what categories exist — the algorithm discovers natural groupings on its own.

## Example
Customer segmentation: An e-commerce company uses K-Means clustering to group customers based on purchase behavior, browsing patterns, and demographics — discovering segments like "budget shoppers," "luxury buyers," and "tech enthusiasts" without predefined labels.

## Spoken Version
"Clustering automatically groups similar data points together — it's how Netflix finds audience segments without knowing in advance what those segments should look like."

## German Workplace Example
"Wir haben Clustering verwendet, um unsere Kunden in verschiedene Segmente zu gruppieren, ohne vorherige Kategorien definieren zu müssen."
