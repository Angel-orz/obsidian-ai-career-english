# Speaking Task — Unsupervised Learning

## Topic: Explain Unsupervised Learning and Customer Segmentation to a Marketing Colleague

**Required duration:** 1-2 minutes

---

## Scenario

You're having coffee with a marketing manager at your German tech company. She knows you work with ML and asks:

> *"I keep hearing about 'unsupervised learning' and 'clustering' for customer segmentation. Can you explain how that actually works — and how it's different from the predictive models you usually build?"*

Your task: Explain unsupervised learning clearly, contrast it with supervised learning, and use customer segmentation as the concrete example. Include at least one German sentence.

---

## Key Points to Cover

1. **Simple definition with contrast** — "Supervised learning predicts with labeled examples; unsupervised learning discovers patterns without labels"
2. **Analogy for clustering** — "Like sorting a pile of mixed Lego bricks by color and shape without anyone telling you the categories"
3. **How it applies to customer segmentation** — "We don't tell the algorithm what segments exist; it finds natural groupings based on behavior"
4. **Why it matters for marketing** — "You might discover segments you never thought of, like 'weekend deal hunters' vs 'full-price brand loyalists'"
5. **One sentence in German** — Include a German phrase naturally
