# 🎙️ Speaking Task — 0617

## Topic: Explain Supervised Learning to a Non-Technical Colleague

**Required duration:** 1-2 minutes

---

## 🎯 Scenario

Imagine you're in a meeting with a product manager in a German tech company. She asks:

> *"I've heard the team mention supervised learning. Can you explain what it is and why we need labeled data for our project?"*

Your task: Explain supervised learning clearly, using an analogy, without jargon. Then explain why labeled data matters for the project.

---

## 📋 Key Points to Cover

1. **Simple definition** — "A way for computers to learn from examples"
2. **Analogy** — Use the "child learning with flashcards" analogy or create your own
3. **Why labeled data matters** — Without correct answers, the model can't learn
4. **Real example** — Relate it to a project/product the PM would recognize
5. **One sentence in German** — Include at least one German sentence naturally

---

## 🎤 Recording Instructions

1. Record yourself using your phone or computer
2. Speak for 1-2 minutes
3. Listen to the recording twice:
   - First time: Focus on **content** — did you cover all key points?
   - Second time: Focus on **delivery** — pace, clarity, filler words
