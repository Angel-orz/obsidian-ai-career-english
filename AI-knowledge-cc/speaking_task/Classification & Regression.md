# Speaking Task — Classification & Regression

## Scenario

> **Setting:** You're in a technical interview for an AI Engineer position at Bosch. The interviewer has just asked: "Can you walk me through the difference between classification and regression, and give me an example from a real project where you had to choose between them?"

> **Your task:** Deliver a confident, structured, 60-90 second answer that demonstrates both technical depth and practical experience. The interviewer is a senior ML engineer — they know the basics. They're testing whether you can explain clearly and think pragmatically about trade-offs.

## Key Points to Cover

- [ ] Start with the one-sentence distinction: classification = categories, regression = numbers
- [ ] Mention at least one algorithm per type (logistic regression for classification, linear regression/gradient boosting for regression)
- [ ] Mention how evaluation differs (F1/confusion matrix vs. RMSE/MAE)
- [ ] Give a concrete example where you had to CHOOSE between the two framings
- [ ] Show you understand the business trade-off, not just the math
- [ ] End with a practical insight or lesson learned
